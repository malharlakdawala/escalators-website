FAQ

Questions leaders ask us first.

Do we have to replace our current systems?
No. We build on the ERP, CRM, HRMS and data platforms you already run. The AI layer sits on top and works through them.
How is this different from buying an AI platform?
A platform gives you tools. We design and ship the whole outcome: the workflow, the experience your teams use and the integration behind it, using whichever tools fit your stack.
How soon will we see something working?
The Friction Audit gives you a ranked plan in about two weeks. Your first workflow typically goes live in production within the Automate stage.
Which AI models do you use?
We are model-agnostic. We choose between commercial and open-source models based on your security, cost and quality needs.
Who owns what you build?
You do. Everything runs in your environment, and your teams keep full access to the workflows, data and code.
What if AI isn't the right answer?
We'll say so. Some bottlenecks are fixed by a process change or a better hand-off, and that is part of what the audit tells you.
