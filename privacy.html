Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. Here is what we collect, why, and the choices you have, in plain language.

Who we are

escalators.ai ("we", "us") helps organizations design and build AI-powered workflows. This policy explains how we collect and use personal information when you visit our website or contact us. Questions? Email hello@escalators.ai.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, work email, company, role and anything you share when you book a call or email us.
  • Usage information: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location (from IP address) and referring site, collected through cookies if you allow them.
  • Client project data: when we work with you, we process data under a separate agreement, not this policy.

How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and schedule calls.
  • To run, secure and improve the website.
  • To send updates you have asked for (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Legal basis

Where laws such as the GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act apply, we rely on your consent (for example, analytics cookies), our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, and contract or legal obligations where relevant.

Who we share it with

Trusted service providers who help us run the business, such as hosting, email, scheduling and analytics tools. They may only use your data to provide their service to us. We may also share information if required by law.

AI and your data

We do not use information submitted through this website, or client data, to train public AI models. Client data is handled under the security and confidentiality terms in each client agreement.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless we have an ongoing relationship or a legal reason to keep it longer.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to or limit how we use it. You can also withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@escalators.ai and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and reputable providers to protect your information. No method is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you of any breach where the law requires it.

International transfers

Our team and providers may be located in different countries. When data moves across borders, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
